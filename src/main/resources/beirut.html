Beirut, the Pearl of the Mediterranean

History, food, culture and sea, all in one unforgettable city.

Plan your trip

Highlights

History

Walk through 5,000 years of Phoenician, Roman, Ottoman and French Mandate heritage.

Food

Share mezze, fresh seafood and warm manakish in one of the world's great food cities.

Culture

Galleries, theatres, live music and street art make the city a creative capital.

Seaside

Stroll the Corniche, swim from rocky coves and watch the sun set over the Mediterranean.

Neighbourhoods

Gemmayzeh

Heritage houses, cafes and bars along a lively hillside street.

Raouche

Cliffside promenade facing the famous Pigeon Rocks, best at sunset.

Downtown

Restored squares, Roman remains, grand mosques and churches, and the marina.

Best time to visit

Spring (April to June)

Warm days, blooming hills and ideal weather for walking the city.

Summer (July to September)

Hot, sunny beach weather and a busy calendar of festivals.

Autumn (October to November)

Warm sea, golden light and fewer crowds.
